# Udacity-SQL-DeforestationExploration

## Project Context

In this project, you operate as a data analyst for **ForestQuery**, an environmental non-profit dedicated to curbing worldwide deforestation and driving public conservation awareness.

To maximize the impact of constrained organizational resources, the Executive Director and leadership team require data-driven answers on two fronts:
- **Areas of Concern**: Pinpointing nations and regions suffering the steepest declines in forest cover.
- **Key Forest Reserves**: Highlighting areas with the largest forest coverage by both total land surface and percentage of overall land area.

These findings directly guide strategic programs, media outreach, and team assignments.

### Analytical Objectives & Scope
To prepare for an upcoming executive review in a few days:
1. **Data Integration**: Aggregate disparate online datasets (forestation statistics, national surface areas, and regional groupings) into a central database.
2. **SQL Investigation**: Query the database to extract insights into shifts across the 1990–2016 timeline.
3. **Leadership Deliverable**: Draft and publish a comprehensive narrative briefing written in complete sentences to guide executive decision-making.
